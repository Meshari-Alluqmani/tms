Questions people ask

Who drives my car?

A driver from our team. You choose the day, the time and the pickup place (home or work), and the driver brings the car back when the job is done.

Is my car covered while your driver has it?

Yes. Every trip is covered by our insurance, from pickup until the car is back with you.

What does it cost?

For individuals, SAR 120 for each driver job, plus the normal fee for the job itself. For companies, all pickups are covered by the annual maintenance contract.

What if I want to do it myself?

Press “I'll do it myself”. One week later we ask if it is done, so nothing is forgotten.

Can you renew my Istimara?

No. Only the car's owner can renew the Istimara, in the Absher app. We remind you in time and tell you what Absher needs first: a valid Fahas, active insurance and no unpaid traffic fines. We can do the Fahas and the insurance for you.

How does buying a car work?

Pick a car, choose delivery or pickup, and pay a deposit to hold it. The rest is paid through Absher’s Dhamen account, so the seller is paid only after the ownership transfer.

What does “Verified” mean on a car's history?

The job was done by our drivers, so we know it happened. Records marked “Owner reported” were added by the owner.
